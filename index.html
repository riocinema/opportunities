RIO FOREVER Senior Project Manager £45,000 pro rata 3 days a week · 12 months +

Help celebrate the Rio’s heritage and shape its future.

RIO FOREVER is a year-long project marking 50 years of the Rio, Hackney’s leading independent cinema, being run by and for its community.

We will celebrate, record and share the Rio’s heritage through community archiving sessions, a wide-ranging participatory public consultation and work with a documentary filmmaker. We will also commission the architectural, condition, accessibility and sustainability work, alongside business and fundraising planning, that will help secure the future of our Grade II listed building.

As Senior Project Manager, you will lead RIO FOREVER day to day, from start to finish. You will own the project plan, timeline and budget, and commission and manage around ten professional briefs phased across the year.

About the role

  • Salary: £45,000 pro rata.
  • Contract: part time, three days a week, for 12 months.
  • Location: Rio Cinema, with some remote working possible.
  • Reports to: the Rio’s Executive Director.

What you will do

  • Deliver the project on time and to budget, maintaining the project plan, timeline and risk register, and reporting progress to the Executive Director and trustees.
  • Procure and manage external consultants, including architects, surveyors, accessibility and sustainability specialists, a community consultation team and an independent evaluator.
  • Manage the project budget and day-to-day relationship with The National Lottery Heritage Fund, including progress reports, payment requests and grant requirements.
  • Plan community archiving and consultation events, working with the Rio’s Operations, Technical and Marketing teams and an external PR agency.
  • Coordinate evaluation and documentation, oversee the final project report and represent the Rio with community partners, sector colleagues and funders.

Who we are looking for

This is a hands-on role for an experienced project manager who has independently led complex projects of comparable scale from start to finish. You will bring strong budget management, careful attention to detail and the confidence to work independently within a small, busy team.

You will have experience of commissioning and managing external consultants, delivering externally funded projects, reporting to funders and developing public events or community engagement in an arts, heritage or cultural organisation. A genuine interest in community histories and a commitment to inclusion and access to the arts are essential.

Experience of National Lottery Heritage Fund projects, community archiving, historic or listed buildings, or capital project development would be welcome. Please read the job description and person specification for the full requirements.

How to apply

Please email Rosie, our Executive Director, at rosie@riocinema.org.uk with your CV and a cover letter of no more than two sides of A4. Use the subject line “Senior Project Manager Application”.

Application deadline: midday (12:00), Friday 4 December 2026.

We warmly welcome applicants from people of all backgrounds, and encourage applications from individuals who bring lived experiences and perspectives that reflect the vibrant, multicultural, and intersectional nature of our communities.

As a community-led organisation, we are particularly interested in applications from people with strong connections to the borough of Hackney.
